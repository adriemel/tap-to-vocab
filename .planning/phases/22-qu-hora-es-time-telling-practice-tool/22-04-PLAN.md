    1. On the home screen, confirm a full-width `🕐 Qué hora es?` button sits directly below `💬 Quién soy yo` and above `🎮 Play Games`, with the same gradient and blue border as the Quién soy yo button.

    3. Confirm the clock card shows an hour reel, a muted `:`, and a minute reel side by side.     **HORA-03 — drag behaviour (desktop mouse)**
    4. Press and drag the hour reel slowly upward — values scroll continuously under the cursor (not one jump per swipe), the reel gains a blue border and grows slightly while you hold, and on release it settles smoothly onto a whole value. Dragging down reverses it.
